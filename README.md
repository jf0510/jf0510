# Hi, I'm John

I'm a backend engineer, indie hacker, and co-founder of [32Tech](https://www.32tech.co).

I've spent years working in startups. My favorite part has always been building products—from the first idea to something I use every day.

At 32Tech, we build apps that make everyday life a little easier:

- [Dashla](https://dashla.32tech.co) — A Tesla dashboard on your phone.
- [Camzy](https://camzy.32tech.co) — Replay, edit, and export Tesla dashcam footage.
- [InsightTube](https://insighttube.32tech.co) — Summaries, translations, and AI conversations for YouTube videos.

There's always something new in the works. I build things I want to use, and hope others will enjoy them too.

You can also find my open-source work here, including [Sill for Android](https://github.com/jf0510/sill-android), an unofficial Android client for viewing and controlling your Mac.
